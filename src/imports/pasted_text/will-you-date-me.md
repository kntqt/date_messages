Create a **romantic, cute, and interactive “Will You Date Me?” website** for my girlfriend named **Jill**.

The website should feel like a small interactive love story, with smooth animations, cute effects, romantic background music (with a mute/unmute button), and a beautiful modern design. Make it responsive for both **mobile and desktop**.

## 💌 PAGE 1 — ENVELOPE INTRO

When Jill first opens the website, show a romantic landing screen.

Display:

> 💌 A Special Message for Jill

In the center, show a **closed animated envelope**.

Below it:

> “I have something special for you…”

Add a button or instruction:

> **Click the envelope to open 💌**

When Jill clicks the envelope:

* Play a smooth envelope-opening animation.
* The letter/message should come out of the envelope.
* Add floating hearts, sparkles, or subtle romantic particles.
* Transition smoothly to the next screen.

---

## 💕 PAGE 2 — THE QUESTION

After opening the envelope, display a romantic message:

> **“Jill, will you go on a date with me?” ❤️**

Add two buttons:

### ❤️ YES

### 💔 NO

Make the YES button attractive and easy to click.

The **NO button should be playful and difficult to click**.

### NO Button Behavior

When Jill tries to move her cursor toward the **NO** button:

* The button should dodge/move to another random position on the screen.
* It should never move outside the visible screen.
* Add a small funny animation whenever it moves.
* Occasionally make it blink or teleport to another location.
* On mobile, where hover doesn't exist, make the NO button move when she tries to tap it.
* The NO button should NOT be permanently disabled; instead, it should playfully dodge the click.
* The YES button must remain stable and clickable.

You can display funny messages when the NO button escapes, such as:

> “Are you sure? 👀”

> “Nice try 😂”

> “You almost got me!”

> “Nope! 😆”

> “Think again, Jill ❤️”

Keep this playful and romantic rather than annoying.

---

* Tailwind CSS
